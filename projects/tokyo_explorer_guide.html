The Metropolis of Unmatched Contrasts

Tokyo, officially Tokyo Metropolis, is the capital and largest city of Japan. Located at the head of Tokyo Bay on the Pacific coast of central Honshu, it is the economic, cultural, and political center of the nation.

Whether you are walking through quiet 1,000-year-old temple gardens in Asakusa or crossing the electric, high-energy neon intersections of Shibuya, Tokyo provides an unforgettable journey where futuristic convenience respects centuries-old traditions.

Essential Tokyo Etiquette

Key cultural rules to keep your journey smooth, polite, and respectful.

Public Behavior

  • Keep voice low on subways & public transport.
  • Avoid walking while eating street food.
  • Smoke only in designated street smoking zones.

No Tipping Rule

  • Tipping is not customary and can cause confusion.
  • High quality service is included by default everywhere.
  • Pass cash with both hands using money trays when provided.

Escalator Manners

  • In Tokyo (Kanto), stand on the left side.
  • Leave the right side open for commuters walking up.
  • Form organized single lines at train doors.

The green JR Yamanote train line loops around Tokyo's entire core, directly connecting Shibuya, Shinjuku, Tokyo Station, Akihabara, and Ueno.
